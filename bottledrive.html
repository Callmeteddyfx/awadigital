About
The Drive

Hello Victoria,

This Community Bottle Drive was created to turn your refundable bottles into meaningful humanitarian support as we launch our website development agency, awadigital.

We are collecting refundable containers across Victoria and donating the proceeds to trusted humanitarian organizations supporting people affected by crises in Sudan and Palestine.

How funds are split:

  • 50% to Sudan humanitarian relief through IRC
  • 50% to Palestine humanitarian relief through UNICEF

This is a community-led initiative focused on transparency, accountability, and direct impact. After the drive, we will publish total amounts raised along with donation receipts on our social media, so everyone can see exactly where the funds went.

How It
Works

  1. 1. Collect

    You collect your recyclable bottles and save them.

  2. 2. Pickup

    You place a pickup order and we come get the bottles from you.

  3. 3. Recycle

    We count and recycle the bottles at the Bottle “Return-It” Depot.

  4. 4. Donate

    We donate the money at our selected donation organization.
